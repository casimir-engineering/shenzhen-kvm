## Shenzhen KVM 1.0.5

### Moving between the computers
- **Any edge works.** Push the pointer past any edge of your screens (left, right, top or bottom) and it goes to the other computer. It comes in on the opposite side there (leave through the right, come in on the left) at the same place along that edge. Coming back works the same way, from any edge of the other screen. The "other computer's screen is…" setting is gone from both windows.
- **Only on purpose.** The pointer crosses when you keep pushing it past the edge, not when it just reaches it. Going up to the menu bar, down to the Dock or a panel, or overshooting a little, keeps it where it is.
- **Corners stay yours.** Near the corners of a screen the pointer never crosses, so hot corners and the buttons in the corners of menu bars and panels work as usual.
- **Dragging stays here.** While you hold a mouse button (dragging a window, a file or a selection), the pointer doesn't cross.
- Edges between two of your own screens work as before: the pointer just moves to your other screen.

## Updating

Shenzhen KVM offers this update by itself within a day, or choose **Check for Updates…** in the app menu. Your link, settings and the permission to use the keyboard and mouse stay as they are.

Update the Linux side too, from the repository (`linux/install.sh`). Until it's updated, the pointer still leaves the Linux computer only through the edge chosen there before.
