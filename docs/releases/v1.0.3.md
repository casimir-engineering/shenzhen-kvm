## Shenzhen KVM 1.0.3

### Fixed
- **No more flicker on the Linux screen.** Changing a setting in either window, or linking the two computers again, used to make the Linux computer's desktop drop its pointer and keyboard from Shenzhen KVM for a moment and pick them up again. Now they stay put.
- Settings that are sent again without a change are no longer applied again.

## Updating

Shenzhen KVM 1.0.2 offers this update by itself within a day, or choose **Check for Updates…** in the app menu. Your link, settings and the permission to use the keyboard and mouse stay as they are.

The fix is on the Linux side: update it from the repository (`linux/install.sh`).
