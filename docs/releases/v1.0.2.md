## Shenzhen KVM 1.0.2

### Fixed
- **The pointer stays where it is when a computer restarts.** Updating Shenzhen KVM, quitting it or restarting either computer used to bring the pointer to the other screen. Now it stays on the screen it was on, and the computer that restarted picks up where it left off: its own pointer stays hidden while the pointer is on the other screen, and its keyboard and mouse keep working there.

## Updating

Shenzhen KVM 1.0.1 offers this update by itself within a day, or choose **Check for Updates…** in the app menu. Your link, settings and the permission to use the keyboard and mouse stay as they are.

The Linux side has the same fix; update it from the repository (`linux/install.sh`).
