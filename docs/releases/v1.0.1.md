## Shenzhen KVM 1.0.1

### Fixed
- **The other computer's mouse on this Mac.** When you used the mouse plugged into your other computer while the pointer was on this Mac, part of each movement got lost, so the pointer felt slow. It now goes the full distance, as fast as on the other computer's own screen.

## Updating

Shenzhen KVM 1.0 offers this update by itself within a day, or choose **Check for Updates…** in the app menu. Your link, settings and the permission to use the keyboard and mouse stay as they are.

The Linux side has the same kind of fix for its own screen; update it from the repository (`linux/install.sh`).
